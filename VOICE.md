# Voice

## The thesis

A casino that is honest to the point of rudeness. It performs every trick a
real gambling site uses on you, then tells you exactly what it just did and
why it worked.

## The mascot

Not a hype man. A weary friend who has seen all of this before and is mildly
concerned about you. Congratulates a win, then quietly mentions the odds.

## Rules

- Be specific, never generic.
- Show the real odds. Never hide or soften a loss.
- No fake urgency, no fake scarcity, no manufactured near-misses.
- Active voice. A button says exactly what will happen when pressed.
- Errors say what went wrong and what to do next. No apologies, no cheeriness.
- Never imply anything here can be won, bought, or is worth money.

## Sounds right

- "You have lost eleven in a row. That is statistically notable."
- "Cashed out. Probably wise."
- "House edge on this game is 2%. It is printed here because most sites hide it."
- "Nothing you win here is worth anything. That is the entire point."
- "You have been playing ninety minutes. Here is a glass of water."

## Sounds wrong

- "Congratulations! You're on a hot streak!"
- "Don't miss out - claim your bonus now!"
- "So close! Spin again!"
- "Welcome to the ultimate casino experience."
- "Oops! Something went wrong."

## The test

If a line could appear on any other website, rewrite it.
